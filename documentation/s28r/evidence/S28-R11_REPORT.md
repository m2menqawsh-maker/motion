## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone **S28-R11** completes the formal decoupling of final video assembly from individual rendering engines. It introduces the **Master Compositor** (`compositor/master-compositor.ts`) and the **Output Normalization Subsystem** (`compositor/output-normalizer.ts`), enabling the video maker platform to ingest intermediate artifacts generated by diverse headless engines (`CanvasRendererAdapter`, `RemotionRendererAdapter`, and future engines) and assemble them into a unified, normalized, broadcast-standard video artifact.

### Architectural Truths Proven:
1. **Engine-Neutral Compositing**: The Master Compositor operates without knowing the internal implementation of any concrete renderer. It consumes standardized `IntermediateArtifact` contracts with verified provenance and media characteristics.
2. **Canonical Timing Authority**: The Master Compositor does NOT invent an independent timeline clock. All scene placements, transition overlaps, and audio cues are derived strictly from the `Canonical VideoDocument` (`BlueprintV2`).
3. **Multi-Engine Assembly**: Successfully renders Scene A with `CanvasRendererAdapter` and Scene B with `RemotionRendererAdapter`, assembling them into a single coherent 1080p MP4.
4. **Deterministic Normalization**: Eliminates resolution differences (720p vs 1080p, 16:9 vs 9:16), framerate variances (60fps vs 30fps), color space differences (`rgba` vs `yuv420p`), and audio layouts (mono 22.05kHz vs stereo 48kHz) with zero duration drift.
5. **Canonical Audio Semantics**: Preserves all **S28-R07** audio plan semantics (voiceover timing, background music looping, dynamic voiceover ducking, and global SFX).
6. **Strict FFmpeg Isolation**: System FFmpeg commands and filtergraphs remain strictly isolated inside `compositor/`. The canonical contracts in `contracts/` remain 100% pure TypeScript / Zod with zero FFmpeg dependencies.
7. **Clean Cancellation & Lifecycle**: AbortSignal cancellation immediately halts subprocesses and sweeps temporary working directories without orphaned disk artifacts.

## 3. Critical Verification Gates

### 3.1 Critical Test — Mixed Engines (`R11-04`)
- **Setup**: Canonical project with Scene 1 rendered via `CanvasRendererAdapter` (2D vector title card) and Scene 2 rendered via `RemotionRendererAdapter` (stat card).
- **Execution**: `MasterCompositor.composite()` ingested both intermediate artifacts, normalized them to 1080p@30fps, and stitched them into a unified video.
- **Verification**: Output MP4 probed with `ffprobe`: exact 1920x1080 resolution, 30 fps, duration $2.0\text{ s}$ ($\Delta = 0\text{ ms}$). **PASS**.

### 3.2 Critical Test — Normalization (`R11-03`)
- **Inputs**:
  - Artifact A: $1280 \times 720$, $30\text{ fps}$, duration $2.0\text{ s}$ (60 frames).
  - Artifact B: $1920 \times 1080$, $60\text{ fps}$, duration $2.0\text{ s}$ (120 frames).
- **Target Profile**: $1920 \times 1080$, $30\text{ fps}$.
- **Verification**: Output video duration exactly $4.0\text{ s}$, framerate exactly $30\text{ fps}$, resolution $1920 \times 1080$, zero frame drift. **PASS**.

### 3.3 Critical Test — Audio Timing & Ducking (`R11-05`)
- **Inputs**: Visual video artifact ($3.0\text{ s}$) + voiceover track ($1.0\text{ s}$, active frames 30–60) + background music ($3.0\text{ s}$, ducking enabled) + SFX ($0.5\text{ s}$).
- **Verification**: Music volume dynamically attenuated during voiceover window ($t = 1.0\text{ s}$ to $2.0\text{ s}$), audio resampled to 48kHz stereo AAC, duration strictly $3.0\text{ s}$. **PASS**.

### 3.4 Critical Test — AV Sync (`R11-06`)
- **Inputs**: 24 fps video + 22.05 kHz mono audio stem resampled to 30 fps and 48 kHz stereo.
- **Verification**: Video duration and audio stream duration match canonical duration with $< 50\text{ ms}$ delta. **PASS**.

### 3.5 Critical Test — Transition Overlap Handling (`R11-07`)
- **Inputs**: Two 2.0s scenes with a 15-frame ($0.5\text{ s}$) crossfade transition.
- **Verification**: Canonical duration authority calculated $(60 + 60) - 15 = 105\text{ frames} = 3.5\text{ s}$. Output video duration probed at $3.5\text{ s}$ ($\Delta < 0.1\text{ s}$). **PASS**.

### 3.6 Critical Test — Cancellation & Cleanup (`R11-09`)
- **Action**: Injected `AbortSignal` before/during compositing.
- **Verification**: Execution immediately threw `[CANCELLED]` error; temporary working directory and partial output files were completely evicted. Zero orphaned files. **PASS**.

### 3.7 Critical Test — Existing QC Integration (`R11-10`)
- **Action**: Ran `runQcForCompositorResult()` on final composed output against canonical blueprint.
- **Verification**: All QC gates passed: file integrity, canonical duration parity ($\Delta < 100\text{ ms}$), resolution compliance, framerate compliance, and AV sync. **PASS**.
