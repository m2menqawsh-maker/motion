# DECISION: Feature Freeze

**Date:** September 2026
**Status:** Active
**Scope:** Entire Codebase

## Decision
All new feature development is strictly frozen until the "Zero-Known-Defects" remediation roadmap is fully completed.

## Rules
- No new features.
- No experimental code.
- No random refactoring outside the remediation roadmap.
- Only critical bug fixes and remediation tasks are allowed, subject to approval.
